How ShieldJob Protects You

Instead of guessing, we use a 4-layer safety check to inspect the job wording, company background, official website, and online footprint.

The 4 Safety Layers

1

1. Pattern Matcher (30%)

Scans text patterns against 17,800+ known legitimate and fraudulent job postings.

2

2. AI Content Inspector (30%)

Spots red flags like fake interview promises, upfront fees, unrealistic salaries, or pressure tactics.

3

3. Website Authenticity (20%)

Visits the employer's official website to confirm real business operations and professional identity.

4

4. Company Reputation (20%)

Checks public presence across LinkedIn, employee reviews, and scam alert databases.

5

5. Unified Safety Score

Combines all 4 layers into a single 0–100 score so you get an instant, trustworthy answer.

6

6. Downloadable Audit Report

Get a clean, detailed PDF report you can save or share with friends and family.

How to Understand Your Safety Score

The final score (0–100) tells you how safe the job opportunity is:

70 – 100: Safe to Apply High confidence that the company and posting are genuine.
50 – 69: Proceed with Caution Mixed signals. Verify company details directly on their official careers page.
0 – 49: Danger — Likely a Scam Matches known scam tricks. Never pay any fee or send identity documents.
