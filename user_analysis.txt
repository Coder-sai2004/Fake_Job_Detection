[031] style=Normal align=None font=None sz=16.0 bold=True | 1. ABSTRACT
[032] style=Normal align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=None bold=None | Recruitment fraud on online job portals inflicts severe financial loss and identity theft on job seekers. Traditional keyword filters fail against sophisticated fraud schemes. This report presents ShieldJob AI, a multi-pillar fraud detection system combining Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning, and Generative Artificial Intelligence. Text features are extracted via TF-IDF with unigrams and bigrams across 17,880 historical job postings and classified using a Balanced Random Forest model. To overcome single-classifier limitations, Google Gemini 2.5 Flash validates contextual red flags, while BeautifulSoup scrapes company domains and search APIs audit social footprints. ShieldJob AI achieves 98.05% accuracy, 79.10% precision, 81.29% recall, and 98.49% ROC-AUC, deployed as a full-stack Flask application and Manifest V3 Chrome Extension.

[039] style=Normal align=None font=None sz=16.0 bold=True | 3. INTRODUCTION
[040] style=Normal align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=None bold=None | The digital transformation of the employment marketplace has revolutionized modern talent acquisition. Online job boards, professional networking platforms such as LinkedIn, and automated career aggregators have drastically reduced friction in connecting job seekers with prospective employers. However, this open digital ecosystem has also fostered an exponential surge in sophisticated recruitment scams, fraudulent job advertisements, and predatory phishing campaigns targeting college graduates, entry-level professionals, and remote job seekers worldwide.
[041] style=Normal align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=None bold=None | Recruitment scams represent a catastrophic threat across multiple vectors: financial extortion disguised as mandatory "security deposits" or "training kits", identity theft through sensitive personal data collection (PAN, Aadhaar, bank credentials), and psychological trauma. Conventional security defenses typically rely on static keyword blacklists or isolated machine learning classifiers operating solely on raw job description text. Such single-dimensional approaches fail when fraudulent actors copy authentic company job templates verbatim, differing only in interview links, fake company domains, or unverified contact information.
[042] style=Normal align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=None bold=True | The Multi-Pillar Verification Paradigm: To overcome the vulnerabilities of single-method detection systems, this project presents ShieldJob AI—a comprehensive, multi-pillar fake job detection and risk assessment system developed during the internship at VedXlence Innovations Pvt. Ltd. ShieldJob AI integrates four independent analytical layers into a unified verification engine:
[043] style=List Bullet align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=None bold=True | 1. Machine Learning Classifier: An optimized Balanced Random Forest model trained on 17,880 historical job postings using TF-IDF unigram and bigram features to detect fraudulent textual syntax.
[044] style=List Bullet align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=None bold=True | 2. Generative AI Contextual Auditor: Google Gemini 2.5 Flash LLM acting as an expert fraud investigator to audit unrealistic salary claims, vague job responsibilities, and covert fee requests.
[045] style=List Bullet align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=None bold=True | 3. Company Website & Domain Analyzer: Automated BeautifulSoup scraping combined with AI verification to evaluate domain authenticity, business purpose, and contact legitimacy.
[046] style=List Bullet align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=None bold=True | 4. Social Media & Public Footprint Verifier: Real-time search engine verification across LinkedIn, Reddit, Glassdoor, and community complaint databases with automated Gemini knowledge fallback.
[047] style=Normal align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=None bold=None | Furthermore, to bridge the gap between academic algorithms and end-user accessibility, ShieldJob AI was implemented as both a high-performance Flask Single-Page Web Application and a Manifest V3 Google Chrome Extension. Job seekers can analyze job postings directly on recruitment portals with a single click, receiving transparent explainability bullets and an intuitive 0–100 Safety Score.

[066] style=Normal align=None font=None sz=13.5 bold=True | 5.2 Data Preprocessing and Feature Extraction (TF-IDF)
[067] style=Normal align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=None bold=None | Job text features from multiple fields (title, company profile, description, requirements, benefits) are concatenated into a unified text corpus. The text undergoes standard text normalization: lowercase conversion, URL tokenization, special character cleaning, and stopword removal. Feature vectorization is performed using Scikit-Learn's TfidfVectorizer with the following verified hyperparameter configuration:
[068] style=List Bullet align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=None bold=True | • max_features = 12,000: Limits vocabulary to the 12,000 most informative textual terms.
[069] style=List Bullet align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=None bold=True | • ngram_range = (1, 2): Extracts both single words (unigrams, e.g., 'wire', 'transfer') and two-word combinations (bigrams, e.g., 'wire transfer', 'data entry', 'immediate joining').
[070] style=List Bullet align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=None bold=True | • sublinear_tf = True: Applies sublinear term-frequency scaling (1 + log(tf)) to prevent excessively frequent words from dominating feature weights.
[071] style=List Bullet align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=None bold=True | • min_df = 2: Prunes rare terms appearing in fewer than 2 documents to eliminate noise.
[072] style=Normal align=None font=None sz=13.5 bold=True | 5.3 Machine Learning Classification (Balanced Random Forest)
[073] style=Normal align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=None bold=None | Due to severe class imbalance (only 4.84% scam samples), standard classifiers tend to achieve high accuracy by simply predicting the majority class (95.16% baseline). To solve this, ShieldJob AI utilizes a Balanced Random Forest Classifier (RandomForestClassifier(n_estimators=100, class_weight='balanced', random_state=42)). The 'balanced' class weighting automatically adjusts weights inversely proportional to class frequencies: w_j = N / (k * n_j). Furthermore, a calibrated decision threshold of 0.24 was established through out-of-fold cross-validation to maximize scam recall while controlling false alarms.
[074] style=Normal align=None font=None sz=13.5 bold=True | 5.4 Gemini AI Contextual Auditing
[075] style=Normal align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=None bold=None | While ML detects statistical n-gram frequencies, sophisticated scammers often write syntactically correct text that mimics real job offers. ShieldJob AI deploys Google Gemini 2.5 Flash as a semantic fraud auditor. The LLM evaluates text for covert extortion indicators: demands for upfront registration or training fees, requests for personal banking details prior to an interview, unrealistically inflated salaries for entry-level work, and vague corporate mission statements. Structured JSON output formats ensure deterministic API parsing and zero hallucination risk.
[076] style=Normal align=None font=None sz=13.5 bold=True | 5.5 Website and Social Presence Verification
[077] style=Normal align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=None bold=None | Scammers frequently register spoofed or shell website domains. The Website Analyzer uses BeautifulSoup to extract live web content, and Gemini evaluates whether the domain reflects genuine business operations or a generic one-page shell. Simultaneously, the Social Media Analyzer queries Brave Search and Google CSE for company LinkedIn profiles, Glassdoor/Indeed employee reviews, and Reddit scam discussions. If live search quotas are unavailable, it seamlessly switches to Gemini AI Knowledge Mode.
[078] style=Normal align=None font=None sz=13.5 bold=True | 5.6 Multi-Pillar Safety Score Aggregation
[079] style=Normal align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=None bold=True | Verified Mathematical Formulation: The overall safety assessment aggregates all four independent evaluation layers into a unified 0–100 Safety Score. The verified scoring formula and classification thresholds implemented in app.py are defined as:
[080] style=Normal align=CENTER (1) font=None sz=11.5 bold=True | Safety Score = (ML_Score × 0.30) + (Gemini_AI_Score × 0.30) + (Website_Score × 0.20) + (Social_Score × 0.20)
[081] style=List Bullet align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=None bold=True | • Score 70 – 100 (Emerald): Legitimate / Safe to Apply — Strong corporate identity, realistic compensation, clean ML/AI audit.
[082] style=List Bullet align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=None bold=True | • Score 50 – 69 (Amber): Suspicious / Proceed with Caution — Limited online footprint, vague responsibilities, or minor red flags.
[083] style=List Bullet align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=None bold=True | • Score 0 – 49 (Rose/Red): High Risk / Likely a Scam — Confirmed fee demands, suspicious domain, severe ML fraud signals.
