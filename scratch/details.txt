P61 [Normal]: 5. METHODOLOGY
P62 [Normal]: The methodology adopted in ShieldJob AI combines supervised machine learning, advanced Natural Language Processing (NLP), large language model (LLM) reasoning, and live digital footprint auditing into an integrated diagnostic pipeline.
P63 [Normal]: 5.1 Dataset Description and Characteristics
P64 [Normal]: The project utilizes the benchmark Employment Scam Aegean Dataset (EMSCAD / processed_jobs) sourced from Kaggle. The dataset contains a total of 17,880 annotated job postings across 18 distinct attributes including job title, company profile, job description, requirements, benefits, employment type, required experience, and the binary target column 'fraudulent' (0 = Legitimate, 1 = Fraudulent).
P66 [Normal]: 5.2 Data Preprocessing and Feature Extraction (TF-IDF)
P67 [Normal]: Job text features from multiple fields (title, company profile, description, requirements, benefits) are concatenated into a unified text corpus. The text undergoes standard text normalization: lowercase conversion, URL tokenization, special character cleaning, and stopword removal. Feature vectorization is performed using Scikit-Learn's TfidfVectorizer with the following verified hyperparameter configuration:
P68 [List Bullet]: • max_features = 12,000: Limits vocabulary to the 12,000 most informative textual terms.
P69 [List Bullet]: • ngram_range = (1, 2): Extracts both single words (unigrams, e.g., 'wire', 'transfer') and two-word combinations (bigrams, e.g., 'wire transfer', 'data entry', 'immediate joining').
P70 [List Bullet]: • sublinear_tf = True: Applies sublinear term-frequency scaling (1 + log(tf)) to prevent excessively frequent words from dominating feature weights.
P71 [List Bullet]: • min_df = 2: Prunes rare terms appearing in fewer than 2 documents to eliminate noise.
P72 [Normal]: 5.3 Machine Learning Classification (Balanced Random Forest)
P73 [Normal]: Due to severe class imbalance (only 4.84% scam samples), standard classifiers tend to achieve high accuracy by simply predicting the majority class (95.16% baseline). To solve this, ShieldJob AI utilizes a Balanced Random Forest Classifier (RandomForestClassifier(n_estimators=100, class_weight='balanced', random_state=42)). The 'balanced' class weighting automatically adjusts weights inversely proportional to class frequencies: w_j = N / (k * n_j). Furthermore, a calibrated decision threshold of 0.24 was established through out-of-fold cross-validation to maximize scam recall while controlling false alarms.
P74 [Normal]: 5.4 Gemini AI Contextual Auditing
P75 [Normal]: While ML detects statistical n-gram frequencies, sophisticated scammers often write syntactically correct text that mimics real job offers. ShieldJob AI deploys Google Gemini 2.5 Flash as a semantic fraud auditor. The LLM evaluates text for covert extortion indicators: demands for upfront registration or training fees, requests for personal banking details prior to an interview, unrealistically inflated salaries for entry-level work, and vague corporate mission statements. Structured JSON output formats ensure deterministic API parsing and zero hallucination risk.
P76 [Normal]: 5.5 Website and Social Presence Verification
P77 [Normal]: Scammers frequently register spoofed or shell website domains. The Website Analyzer uses BeautifulSoup to extract live web content, and Gemini evaluates whether the domain reflects genuine business operations or a generic one-page shell. Simultaneously, the Social Media Analyzer queries Brave Search and Google CSE for company LinkedIn profiles, Glassdoor/Indeed employee reviews, and Reddit scam discussions. If live search quotas are unavailable, it seamlessly switches to Gemini AI Knowledge Mode.
P78 [Normal]: 5.6 Multi-Pillar Safety Score Aggregation
P79 [Normal]: Verified Mathematical Formulation: The overall safety assessment aggregates all four independent evaluation layers into a unified 0–100 Safety Score. The verified scoring formula and classification thresholds implemented in app.py are defined as:
P80 [Normal]: Safety Score = (ML_Score × 0.30) + (Gemini_AI_Score × 0.30) + (Website_Score × 0.20) + (Social_Score × 0.20)
P81 [List Bullet]: • Score 70 – 100 (Emerald): Legitimate / Safe to Apply — Strong corporate identity, realistic compensation, clean ML/AI audit.
P82 [List Bullet]: • Score 50 – 69 (Amber): Suspicious / Proceed with Caution — Limited online footprint, vague responsibilities, or minor red flags.
P83 [List Bullet]: • Score 0 – 49 (Rose/Red): High Risk / Likely a Scam — Confirmed fee demands, suspicious domain, severe ML fraud signals.
