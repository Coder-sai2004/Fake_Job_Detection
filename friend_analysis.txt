[097] style=List Paragraph align=CENTER (1) font=None sz=20.0 bold=True | Abstract
[098] <EMPTY LINE>
[099] style=Normal align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=12.0 bold=None | The Student Permission and Leave Management System is a web-based application developed to automate and simplify the process of managing student leave and permission requests within educational institutions. Traditional paper-based leave systems are time-consuming, prone to errors, and lack transparency in the approval process. This system provides a centralized digital platform where students can submit leave or permission requests and track their status in real time.
[100] <EMPTY LINE>
[101] style=Normal align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=12.0 bold=None | The system implements a role-based workflow involving Students, Faculty (Proctors), Heads of Departments (HODs), Wardens, Principals, and Administrators. Once a student submits a request, it is automatically routed to the assigned proctor for review. After proctor approval, the request is forwarded to the HOD, and for hostel students, it is further sent to the Warden for final approval. The system also supports direct HOD approval when the proctor is unavailable, ensuring uninterrupted processing of requests.
[102] <EMPTY LINE>
[103] style=Normal align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=12.0 bold=None | In addition to leave management, the platform includes an Announcement Management Module that enables administrators, HODs, faculty members, and wardens to share important notices with specific groups of students or staff. Announcements can include PDF documents, images, and other attachments, ensuring effective communication across the institution. The system also maintains complete records of requests, approvals, and announcement activities for future reference and reporting.
[104] <EMPTY LINE>
[105] style=Normal align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=12.0 bold=None | The application is built using React.js for the frontend, Express.js for the backend, and PostgreSQL (Supabase) as the database. By digitizing leave approvals and institutional communication, the Student Permission and Leave Management System reduces paperwork, improves transparency, enhances efficiency, and provides a secure and user-friendly solution for academic administration.

[138] style=List Paragraph align=CENTER (1) font=None sz=20.0 bold=True | Introduction
[139] <EMPTY LINE>
[140] style=Normal align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=12.0 bold=None | Educational institutions handle a large number of student leave and permission requests on a daily basis. Traditionally, these requests are managed through paper forms and manual approval processes, which often lead to delays, miscommunication, loss of records, and increased administrative workload. Students are required to visit multiple authorities to obtain approvals, while faculty members and administrators face difficulties in maintaining and tracking records efficiently. As educational institutions continue to adopt digital technologies, there is a growing need for an automated system that can streamline these processes and improve overall efficiency.
[141] <EMPTY LINE>
[142] style=Normal align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=12.0 bold=None | The Student Permission and Leave Management System is a web-based application designed to digitize and automate the entire leave and permission approval workflow. The system provides a centralized platform where students can submit requests online, monitor their approval status, and receive timely updates. Faculty members, Heads of Departments (HODs), Wardens, Principals, and Administrators can review, approve, reject, or forward requests based on their roles and responsibilities. By eliminating manual paperwork and introducing a structured approval workflow, the system ensures transparency, accountability, and faster decision-making.
[143] <EMPTY LINE>
[144] style=Normal align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=12.0 bold=None | In addition to leave management, the system includes an announcement module that enables authorized users to share important notices, circulars, and updates with students and staff. The platform supports role-based access control, secure authentication, attachment handling, and centralized record management. This ensures that information is delivered efficiently to the intended audience while maintaining data security and integrity.
[145] <EMPTY LINE>
[146] style=Normal align=JUSTIFY (3) font=None sz=12.0 bold=None | The motivation behind this project is to create a reliable, user-friendly, and scalable solution that improves communication and administrative efficiency within educational institutions. By leveraging modern web technologies such as React.js, Express.js, and PostgreSQL, the system provides a secure and responsive environment for managing student permissions, leave requests, and institutional announcements. The proposed solution not only reduces administrative burden but also enhances the overall experience for students and staff by providing a transparent and efficient digital platform.
