  • Layout: Screen Capture on Canvas + Camera in a bottom-right rectangle (Picture-in-Picture).
  • Hotkeys: Set F9 to Start/Stop Recording and F10 to switch between Full Facecam and Screen Share.
  • Audio: Keep mic volume in the green/yellow zone (-12dB to -6dB).
  • Browser Setup: Fullscreen browser (F11), clean dark theme, zoom at 100% or 110%.
